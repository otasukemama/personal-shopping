A few things we can't ship

International shipping rules are stricter than most people expect. Common examples that usually can't be sent overseas:

  • Spare lithium batteries or power banks (batteries built into a device are often fine)
  • Spray cans, perfume, nail polish, and other flammable liquids
  • Fireworks and anything explosive
  • Alcohol (sometimes possible, with restrictions)
  • High-value precious metals or jewelry
  • Fresh food and perishables

The exact rules depend on the item and your country, so we'll flag anything that looks like a problem when we send your quote — before you pay for anything.

Japanese Lessons

Lessons built around you, not a textbook.

One-to-one, over Zoom, taught almost entirely in Japanese — the way you'd pick up a language by living it, not by memorizing rules about it.

Get started

How it works

  1. 1

    A written check-in, in your own language

    Before your first lesson, tell us as much as you can about your goals and current level — in whatever language is easiest for you. The more detail, the better we can prepare.

  2. 2

    A plan shaped around that

    Want to get by comfortably on a trip? Speak more fluently? Just build up conversation practice and get comfortable talking? Based on your goal, we'll suggest materials and an approach, and settle on a direction together before your first lesson.

  3. 3

    Adjusted as you go

    As your Japanese improves (or your goals change), the lessons change with you.

Format

Live, one-to-one lessons over Zoom — no groups, no fixed textbook pace.

Taught in Japanese

We speak Japanese only — it's the best way to actually improve. If you ever get stuck, English or a translation tool is completely fine. The goal is to enjoy the lesson while you learn.
